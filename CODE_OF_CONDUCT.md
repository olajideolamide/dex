# Code of Conduct

DEX is an open-source project maintained by people. We want participation to be safe and welcoming for everyone.

## Our Pledge

We commit to providing a harassment-free experience for all contributors and community members, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in speech and actions
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for the community and the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory remarks
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers. The simplest path is to contact maintainers via GitHub Discussions or another private channel you already have with them. Avoid escalating in public threads.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning, temporary bans, or permanent bans from project participation.
